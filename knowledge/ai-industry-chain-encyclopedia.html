From 3 nanometers to 1 gigawatt从 3 纳米 到 1 吉瓦

Every token a model emits is the far end of a chain that starts with a tin droplet vapourised by a laser and ends with a substation. This manual walks that chain one layer at a time — what each layer physically does, the equations that govern it, who builds it, and why it becomes the bottleneck.模型吐出的每一个 token，都是一条长链的末端——这条链始于一颗被激光气化的锡液滴，终于一座变电站。本手册逐层走完这条链：每一层在物理上究竟做什么、支配它的方程是什么、由谁建造，以及它为什么会成为瓶颈。

The three-bucket model — upstream, midstream, downstream — is fine for a slide and useless for reasoning. It puts a photoresist chemist and a data-centre electrician in the same box, and it hides the fact that the binding constraint on the whole industry moves. In 2023 it was GPU allocation. In 2024 it was CoWoS packaging capacity. Through 2025 it moved to HBM supply and grid interconnection. Each time, people who only had three buckets were surprised.上游、中游、下游这个三分法，放在一页幻灯片里没问题，用来推理则毫无用处。它把光刻胶化学家和数据中心电气工程师塞进同一个盒子，也掩盖了一个事实：真正制约整个行业的那个约束是会移动的。2023 年是 GPU 配额。2024 年是 CoWoS 封装产能。到 2025 年，它转移到了 HBM 供应与电网并网。每一次，手里只有三个盒子的人都会措手不及。

A better mental model is a stack of conversion layers. Each layer takes something scarce from below, does one irreducible physical or logical transformation, and hands a cleaner abstraction upward. Sand becomes wafers. Wafers become dies. Dies plus memory become packages. Packages become modules, racks, halls, campuses. Then the units stop being metres and start being FLOPs, tokens, and dollars. That boundary — where the discipline changes from physics to computer science — is the single most important line on the map, and it is why this manual splits into four zones.更好的心智模型是一叠转换层。每一层从下面取走某种稀缺之物，完成一次不可再分的物理或逻辑变换，再向上交付一层更干净的抽象。沙子变成晶圆。晶圆变成裸片。裸片加上内存变成封装体。封装体变成模组、机柜、机房、园区。再往上，计量单位就不再是米，而是 FLOP、token 和美元。这条边界——学科从物理学切换到计算机科学的地方——是这张地图上最重要的一条线，也是本手册划分为四个区带的原因。

A layer is worth naming when it has its own failure mode, its own lead time, and its own set of people who lose sleep over it.一个层级值得被单独命名，当且仅当它有自己的失效模式、自己的交付周期，以及自己一群为它睡不着觉的人。

Two clocks, permanently out of sync两只永远对不上的时钟

The single most useful thing to hold in your head: the layers run on different clocks and cannot be resynchronised. A substation interconnection request queues for three to seven years. A leading-edge fab is a three-year, $20–30 billion commitment. HBM capacity is sold out roughly a year forward. Meanwhile a serving-engine optimisation can cut cost per token by 40% in a quarter, and a post-training recipe change can move a benchmark in a fortnight.最值得记住的一句话是：各层运行在不同的时钟上，而且无法重新对齐。一次变电站并网申请要排队三到七年。一座先进制程晶圆厂是为期三年、200–300 亿美元的承诺。HBM 产能大约提前一年就已售罄。与此同时，一次推理引擎优化可以在一个季度内把单 token 成本砍掉 40%，一次后训练配方调整可以在两周内推动一个基准分数。

So the fast layers keep improving the efficiency of an asset base that was sized years earlier under different assumptions. Sometimes that shows up as a shortage — the models got good faster than the megawatts arrived. Sometimes it shows up as stranded capacity — an efficiency win landed and suddenly you need fewer GPUs for the same product. Both are normal. Neither proves a bubble or a boom on its own.于是，快的那几层不断提升一批资产的效率，而这批资产是若干年前、在完全不同的假设下确定规模的。有时这表现为短缺——模型变强的速度快过兆瓦到位的速度。有时它表现为闲置产能——一次效率提升落地，同样的产品突然只需要更少的 GPU。两种情况都很正常。单凭其中任何一种，都不能证明泡沫或繁荣。

Reading heuristic阅读法则

When you see an AI headline, do three things before forming an opinion: (1) place it on a layer, (2) ask which clock it runs on, (3) ask whether it changes the binding constraint or a slack one. An efficiency gain in a slack layer is a press release. An efficiency gain in the binding layer resets the industry.看到一条 AI 新闻时，在形成观点之前先做三件事：(1) 把它定位到某一层，(2) 问它跑在哪只时钟上，(3) 问它改变的是正在起约束作用的那个瓶颈，还是一个本来就有余量的环节。发生在宽松环节的效率提升是一篇新闻稿；发生在紧约束环节的效率提升会重置整个行业。

1.1  Why 13.5 nanometres, and why it is so hard1.1 为什么是 13.5 纳米，又为什么如此之难

Optical resolution is governed by the Rayleigh criterion, CD = k₁ · λ / NA. To print smaller features you shrink the wavelength λ, raise the numerical aperture NA, or squeeze the process factor k₁ with tricks like multiple patterning and optical proximity correction. The industry rode 193 nm deep-ultraviolet light for two decades, buying resolution with immersion optics and by exposing the same layer three or four times.光学分辨率由瑞利判据决定：CD = k₁ · λ / NA。要印出更小的图形，你只能缩短波长 λ、提高数值孔径 NA，或者用多重曝光、光学邻近效应修正之类的手段去压低工艺因子 k₁。整个行业靠 193 nm 深紫外光走了二十年，用浸没式光学和把同一层曝光三四次来换取分辨率。

Going below that meant a jump to extreme ultraviolet at 13.5 nm. That specific number is not a design choice — it is where molybdenum/silicon multilayer Bragg mirrors have a usable reflectance peak. And that constraint cascades into everything else: at 13.5 nm, every material absorbs. No lenses, no air, no conventional masks. The whole light path must be reflective, in hard vacuum, off mirrors polished to sub-nanometre figure error — if one were scaled to the size of Germany, its tallest bump would be under a millimetre.要再往下走，就必须跳到 13.5 nm 的极紫外。这个具体数值不是设计选择——它是钼/硅多层布拉格反射镜存在可用反射率峰值的地方。而这个约束会向下级联到一切：在 13.5 nm 波段，所有材料都吸收光。没有透镜，没有空气，也没有传统掩模。整条光路必须是反射式的，处在高真空中，反射面被抛光到亚纳米级的面形误差——如果把其中一面镜子放大到德国那么大，它最高的凸起也不到一毫米。

2.2  Why chiplets stopped being optional2.2 芯粒为什么不再是可选项

Three forces collided. First, the reticle limit: you cannot expose more than about 858 mm² at once, and frontier accelerators want more area than that. Second, yield: defect probability scales with area, so one enormous die is far more expensive per good transistor than several smaller ones (see §03.3). Third, SRAM has largely stopped shrinking — logic keeps scaling but cache does not, so putting cache on an expensive leading-edge die wastes the most valuable silicon on the wafer.三股力量同时撞在了一起。第一是掩模版尺寸上限：单次曝光最多约 858 mm²，而前沿加速器想要的面积超过这个数。第二是良率：缺陷概率随面积增长，因此一颗巨大裸片按每颗好晶体管计算，要比若干颗小裸片贵得多（见 §03.3）。第三是 SRAM 基本已经不再缩小——逻辑还在继续微缩，缓存却没有，于是把缓存放在昂贵的先进制程裸片上，等于在浪费整片晶圆上最值钱的硅。

The answer is disaggregation: put compute on the newest node, put I/O and analogue on a cheap mature node, put cache on its own die, and stitch them together with advanced packaging (§04). UCIe standardises the die-to-die link so that, in principle, chiplets from different vendors can interoperate — a genuinely new kind of supply chain.答案是解耦：把计算放在最新制程上，把 I/O 和模拟电路放在便宜的成熟制程上，把缓存单独做成一颗裸片，再用先进封装把它们缝合起来（§04）。UCIe 把裸片间互连标准化，原则上让不同厂商的芯粒可以互操作——这是一种真正意义上的新型供应链。

Die-to-die is not free跨裸片通信不是免费的

Crossing a package boundary costs energy and latency that staying on-die does not:越过封装边界，要付出留在片内时不必付出的能量与时延代价：

on-die SRAM access ≈ 0.1–1 pJ/bit  ·  die-to-die (UCIe/organic) ≈ 0.3–0.5 pJ/bit  ·  HBM ≈ 3–5 pJ/bit  ·  off-package SerDes ≈ 5–10 pJ/bit

3.2  Transistor architecture: three eras3.2 晶体管结构：三个时代

Classical planar transistors lost control of the channel as gate lengths shrank — current leaked when the device was supposed to be off. FinFET (volume from ~22 nm, 2011) stood the channel up as a fin so the gate wrapped it on three sides, restoring electrostatic control and enabling a decade of scaling. You add drive strength by adding fins, which quantises design choices.随着栅长缩小，经典平面晶体管失去了对沟道的控制——器件本该关断时电流仍在泄漏。FinFET（约 22 nm、2011 年起量产）把沟道立起来做成鳍状，让栅极从三面包住它，恢复了静电控制，从而支撑了十年的微缩。要提高驱动能力只能增加鳍的数量，这让设计选择变成了离散量。

Gate-all-around nanosheet transistors stack horizontal silicon sheets and wrap the gate on all four sides. Two practical wins: better short-channel control at shorter gate lengths, and continuously tunable drive strength — you widen the sheet instead of adding a whole fin. The cost is process complexity: inner spacers, channel release etch, and a large increase in ALD steps. Samsung shipped GAA first; TSMC's 2 nm generation and Intel's 18A moved the mainstream there.环栅纳米片晶体管把水平硅片层层堆叠，让栅极从四面完全包围沟道。两个实际收益：在更短栅长下有更好的短沟道控制，以及可连续调节的驱动能力——你可以加宽纳米片，而不必整根整根地加鳍。代价是工艺复杂度：内侧隔离层、沟道释放刻蚀，以及 ALD 步骤的大幅增加。三星最先量产 GAA；台积电的 2 nm 一代与英特尔的 18A 把它带进了主流。

Backside power delivery (Intel PowerVia, and equivalents elsewhere) moves the power rails to the underside of the wafer, so the front-side metal stack carries only signals. Two benefits: less IR drop on a high-current die, and freer routing above the transistors. It requires wafer thinning and bonding — borrowing techniques from advanced packaging, and blurring the line between §03 and §04.背面供电（英特尔的 PowerVia 及各家的对应方案）把电源轨移到晶圆背面，于是正面的金属层只需要承载信号。两个好处：大电流裸片上的 IR 压降更小，晶体管上方的布线也更自由。代价是需要晶圆减薄与键合——这些技术借自先进封装，也让 §03 与 §04 之间的界线变得模糊。

3.3  Yield: the equation that shapes the industry3.3 良率：塑造整个行业的那个方程

Random defects land on a wafer roughly as a Poisson process. If a die has area A and the defect density is D₀, the fraction of dies with zero killer defects is:随机缺陷落在晶圆上的分布大致服从泊松过程。若一颗裸片面积为 A、缺陷密度为 D₀，则不含致命缺陷的裸片比例为：

Poisson yield model泊松良率模型

4.1  Why memory bandwidth, not FLOPs, sets the ceiling4.1 为什么定天花板的是内存带宽，而不是 FLOPs

Compute has improved far faster than the ability to feed it. Peak arithmetic throughput on accelerators has risen by orders of magnitude per decade; DRAM bandwidth has not kept pace. The gap is the memory wall, and it is measured with arithmetic intensity: how many FLOPs you do per byte you move.计算能力的进步远快于喂饱它的能力。加速器的峰值算术吞吐每十年提升若干数量级，DRAM 带宽却没有跟上。这道差距就是内存墙，它的度量方式是算术强度：每搬运一字节，你能做多少次 FLOP。

The break-even point临界平衡点
machine balance = peak FLOP/s ÷ memory bandwidth

For a modern accelerator this lands in the region of several hundred FLOPs per byte. Any kernel with lower arithmetic intensity than that is memory-bound — you are paying for arithmetic units that sit idle. Big matrix multiplications with large batch sizes clear the bar comfortably. Single-sequence autoregressive decoding does not, and never will (§12).对现代加速器而言，这个值大致落在每字节几百 FLOP的区间。任何算术强度低于该值的算子都是内存受限的——你付了钱买的算术单元正在空转。大批量的大矩阵乘法能轻松越过这道线。单序列的自回归解码越不过去，而且永远越不过去（§12）。

HBM exists to move that break-even point. Instead of a narrow, fast bus to distant DIMMs, you stack DRAM dies vertically, drill thousands of through-silicon vias straight down through them, and place the stacks millimetres from the compute die on a shared silicon interposer. The interface becomes absurdly wide (1024 bits per stack, rising to 2048 with HBM4) and comparatively slow per pin — which is exactly the right trade, because wide-and-slow is far more energy-efficient per bit than narrow-and-fast.HBM 存在的意义就是挪动这个临界点。它不再用一条又窄又快的总线去连接远处的内存条，而是把 DRAM 裸片垂直堆叠、打上数千个直穿而下的硅通孔，再把这些堆栈放在共享硅中介层上、距离计算裸片仅几毫米处。接口因此变得极宽（HBM3E 每堆栈 1024 位，HBM4 提升到 2048 位），单引脚速率反而相对慢——而这恰恰是正确的取舍，因为“宽而慢”在每比特能效上远优于“窄而快”。

5.1  Anatomy5.1 解剖

A GPU is a throughput machine, not a latency machine. It hides memory latency by having thousands of threads in flight and switching between them for free. Concretely, on a large modern part you have on the order of a hundred-plus streaming multiprocessors (AMD calls them compute units), each containing:GPU 是一台吞吐机器，不是一台低时延机器。它靠同时保有数千个在飞线程、并且在它们之间零成本切换，来隐藏内存时延。具体地说，在一颗大型现代芯片上，你会有一百多个流式多处理器（AMD 称之为计算单元），每一个里面包含：

  • Tensor / matrix cores — fixed-function units that consume small matrix tiles and emit a matrix multiply-accumulate every few cycles. Essentially all of the advertised FLOPs live here; the general-purpose ALUs contribute a small fraction.张量 / 矩阵核——固定功能单元，吃进小块矩阵分片，每隔几个周期吐出一次矩阵乘加。厂商标称的 FLOPs 基本全部来自这里；通用 ALU 只贡献很小一部分。
  • A very large register file — hundreds of kilobytes per SM, larger than the L1 cache, because thousands of resident threads each need private state.一个非常大的寄存器堆——每个 SM 数百 KB，比 L1 缓存还大，因为数千个驻留线程各自都需要私有状态。
  • Shared memory / L1 — software-managed scratchpad. Getting tiles into it, and keeping them there, is what a good GEMM kernel actually does.共享内存 / L1——由软件管理的暂存区。把分片搬进去，并让它们留在里面，就是一个好的 GEMM 算子真正在做的事。
  • Asynchronous copy engines — DMA that moves tiles from HBM into shared memory while the tensor cores work on the previous tile. Overlapping this is most of the performance.异步拷贝引擎——在张量核处理上一块分片时，用 DMA 把下一块分片从 HBM 搬进共享内存。让这两件事重叠起来，就构成了性能的大部分。

Surrounding the SMs: a large shared L2, the HBM controllers and PHYs, the scale-up interconnect (NVLink / Infinity Fabric / ICI), PCIe, media engines, and — increasingly — dedicated hardware for things like tensor memory and block-scaled numeric formats.环绕 SM 的还有：一块大的共享 L2、HBM 控制器与 PHY、纵向扩展互连（NVLink / Infinity Fabric / ICI）、PCIe、媒体引擎，以及越来越多的专用硬件，例如张量内存和块缩放数值格式的支持单元。

    Heuristics (length, symbol ratios, boilerplate) followed by learned classifiers that score documents for whether they resemble text a knowledgeable person wrote carefully.     Decide the proportions: how much code, how much maths, how many languages, how much of each domain — and how those proportions change over the run.   7. Tokenisation and packing分词与打包

Synthetic data, honestly老实说说合成数据

Generating training data with a model is now routine and clearly works in domains with a verifier: mathematics you can check, code you can execute, problems with known answers. The generator proposes, the verifier filters, and the filtered set is genuinely new signal. The dividing line is verifiability, not "synthetic versus real".用模型生成训练数据如今已是常规操作，而且在存在验证器的领域明确有效：可以核对的数学、可以执行的代码、有已知答案的问题。生成器负责提出，验证器负责筛选，筛过之后的集合是真正的新信号。而在没有验证器的地方——开放式写作、品味、判断——它就弱得多，因为模型只能重新分配它已经相信的东西。分界线是可验证性，而不是“合成还是真实”。

10.2  Why kernels matter: the flash-attention lesson10.2 算子为什么重要：flash-attention 这一课

Naive attention materialises an N × N score matrix in HBM. That makes both memory and time quadratic in sequence length, and — crucially — makes the operation memory-bound, so it runs far below peak. The fix was not a new algorithm in the mathematical sense: it computes exactly the same function. It was a scheduling change — tile the computation, keep tiles in on-chip memory, use the online-softmax trick so you never need the full matrix at once, and recompute cheaply in the backward pass instead of storing.朴素注意力会在 HBM 中实体化一个 N × N 的分数矩阵。这让内存和时间都随序列长度呈二次增长，而且——关键在于——让这个操作变成内存受限，于是运行速度远低于峰值。修复方案在数学意义上并不是一个新算法：它计算的是完全相同的函数。它是一次调度上的改变——把计算切成分片、让分片留在片上内存、用在线 softmax 技巧从而不必一次性持有完整矩阵，并在反向传播时用便宜的重算取代存储。

What changed改变了什么
HBM traffic: O(N²) → O(N² / M) for on-chip tile size M  ·  memory for the score matrix: O(N²) → O(N)

Same mathematics, several times the speed, and long context suddenly affordable. Almost every important systems win in AI has this shape: not fewer operations, but fewer trips down the memory hierarchy.数学完全相同，速度快了数倍，长上下文也突然变得负担得起。AI 中几乎每一次重要的系统层面胜利都是这个形状：不是更少的运算，而是更少地沿内存层级往下跑。

10.3  Running at 100,000 ranks10.3 在十万个进程上运行

A synchronous training job is one distributed process with a barrier at every step. Three failure modes dominate, and each has its own countermeasure:一个同步训练作业，是一个每一步都有栅栏的分布式进程。有三种失效模式占据主导，每一种都有自己的对策：

  • Hard failures. A node dies. Handled by frequent checkpointing (§09), hot spares, and elastic restart that rebuilds the process group without re-queueing the job. Best-in-class systems restart in minutes; naive ones lose hours.硬故障。某个节点挂了。对策是高频检查点（§09）、热备节点，以及不必重新排队就能重建进程组的弹性重启。一流系统在几分钟内恢复；朴素做法要损失几个小时。
  • Stragglers. One rank runs 10% slow — a thermally throttled part, a degraded optical link, a noisy neighbour — and because of the barrier, the entire cluster runs 10% slow. Detection has to be continuous and automatic; you cannot find this by looking.掉队者。某个进程慢了 10%——可能是一颗因过热降频的芯片、一条劣化的光链路，或者一个吵闹的邻居——而由于栅栏的存在，整个集群都慢了 10%。检测必须持续且自动；靠人盯是发现不了的。
  • Silent data corruption. At this scale, occasional hardware produces wrong answers without reporting an error. It shows up as a mysterious loss spike or a NaN thousands of steps later. Countermeasures are periodic determinism checks, cross-rank validation of critical reductions, and burn-in screening of new hardware.静默数据损坏。在这个规模下，偶尔会有硬件给出错误结果却不报任何错。它表现为几千步之后一次莫名其妙的 loss 尖刺或者一个 NaN。对策包括周期性确定性校验、对关键归约做跨进程交叉验证，以及对新硬件做老化筛选。
MFU is the number to ask for该问的数字是 MFU

Anyone can quote peak FLOPs. The honest metric is model FLOPs utilisation: useful model FLOPs divided by peak, over the wall-clock of a real run including restarts. Dense pre-training at 35–55% is respectable. Mixture-of-experts training is typically lower because of all-to-all. Reinforcement-learning post-training can be far lower again, because generation and training phases alternate and each leaves the other's hardware idle. When someone quotes a cluster in "exaflops", ask what MFU they sustain — the answer is more informative than the headline.峰值 FLOPs 谁都能报。诚实的指标是模型算力利用率：在一次包含重启在内的真实运行的墙钟时间里，有效模型 FLOP 除以峰值。稠密预训练能到 35–55% 就算体面。混合专家训练通常更低，因为有 all-to-all。强化学习后训练可能还要低得多，因为生成阶段与训练阶段交替进行，各自都让对方的硬件空转。当有人用“exaflops”来描述一个集群时，去问他们能持续保持多少 MFU——这个答案比那个标称数字有信息量得多。

That minimises loss for a fixed training budget.   2. Mid-training / annealing中期训练 / 退火

    Continue on a re-weighted, higher-quality mixture while decaying the learning rate. 12.2  The KV cache, which is the real memory problem12.2 KV 缓存，才是真正的内存问题

Every token generated must attend to every previous token, so their key and value vectors are cached rather than recomputed. That cache grows linearly with context and with the number of concurrent users, and it competes for exactly the same HBM the weights live in.生成的每一个 token 都必须注意到此前所有 token，因此它们的 key 与 value 向量被缓存下来而不是重算。这份缓存随上下文长度和并发用户数线性增长，而且它抢占的正是权重所在的那块 HBM。

KV cache sizeKV 缓存大小
bytes/token = 2 × layers × kv_heads × head_dim × bytes_per_element

For a 70 B-class model with 80 layers, grouped-query attention with 8 KV heads, head dim 128, in fp16:以一个 70 B 级模型为例：80 层、分组查询注意力 8 个 KV 头、头维度 128、fp16：

2 × 80 × 8 × 128 × 2 = 327,680 bytes ≈ 320 KB per token → a 32k-token context is ≈10.5 GB for one sequence

Without grouped-query attention — 64 KV heads instead of 8 — the same context would need ≈84 GB. GQA is not a minor optimisation; it is the change that made long-context serving economically possible at all.如果不用分组查询注意力——64 个 KV 头而不是 8 个——同样的上下文需要约 84 GB。GQA 不是一项小优化；正是它让长上下文服务在经济上第一次成为可能。

12.3  The serving optimisation stack12.3 推理服务的优化栈

Continuous (in-flight) batching is the single largest win in modern serving. Static batching makes every request in a batch wait for the longest one to finish; continuous batching evicts finished sequences and admits new ones at every decoding step, so the batch stays full. Typical effect: several times the throughput at the same latency.连续批处理（in-flight batching）是现代推理服务中收益最大的单项优化。静态批处理会让批里的每个请求都等最长的那个跑完；连续批处理则在每一步解码时淘汰已完成的序列、放入新的序列，让这一批始终是满的。典型效果：在同等时延下把吞吐提升数倍。

The remaining tension is a genuine one — larger batches give better throughput and worse per-user latency. Every serving system exposes this as a scheduling policy, and every product decision about "how fast should this feel" is really a decision about where to sit on that curve.剩下的那份张力是真实存在的——更大的批带来更好的吞吐和更差的单用户时延。每一个推理服务系统都会把它暴露成一项调度策略，而每一个关于“这东西应该感觉有多快”的产品决策，实质上都是在决定你要坐在这条曲线的哪个位置。

Paged attention applies the operating-system insight to the KV cache: allocate it in fixed-size blocks with an indirection table instead of one contiguous reservation per sequence. Fragmentation and over-reservation waste largely disappear, and blocks can be shared between sequences with a common prefix.分页注意力（paged attention）把操作系统的洞见用在了 KV 缓存上：用固定大小的块加一张间接映射表来分配，而不是为每条序列预留一整段连续空间。碎片和过度预留造成的浪费基本消失，而且拥有共同前缀的序列之间还可以共享这些块。

Prefix caching then becomes free money. System prompts, few-shot examples, a long document being asked about repeatedly, an agent's accumulating history — all are shared prefixes. Cache them and prefill cost for those tokens goes to nearly zero. For agentic workloads, where each step re-sends a growing transcript, this is the difference between viable and not.于是前缀缓存就成了白捡的钱。系统提示词、少样本示例、被反复提问的同一份长文档、智能体不断累积的历史记录——这些全都是共享前缀。把它们缓存下来，这部分 token 的预填充成本就趋近于零。对于每一步都要重发一份不断变长的对话记录的智能体负载来说，这就是可行与不可行的分界。

Speculative decoding exploits the fact that decode is bandwidth-bound and therefore has idle arithmetic. A cheap drafter — a small model, a few extra prediction heads, or even an n-gram lookup over the prompt — proposes k tokens; the full model verifies all k in a single forward pass, which costs almost the same as verifying one. Accepted tokens are free.投机解码利用了“解码受带宽限制、因而算术单元闲置”这一事实。一个廉价的起草者——一个小模型、几个额外的预测头，甚至只是在提示词上做 n-gram 查表——先提出 k 个 token；完整模型再用一次前向把这 k 个全部验证掉，代价几乎与验证一个相同。被接受的 token 等于白拿。

Speed-ups of roughly 1.5–3× are typical, entirely dependent on acceptance rate, and the output distribution is provably unchanged when the acceptance test is done correctly. It is one of the rare optimisations with no quality cost.常见加速比大约 1.5–3 倍，完全取决于接受率；而且只要接受判据实现正确，可以证明输出分布保持不变。这是极少数没有质量代价的优化之一。

Weight quantisation to FP8, INT8 or 4-bit cuts both the bytes read per token and the HBM needed to hold the model. Since decode is bandwidth-bound, halving the weight bytes very nearly halves decode latency. The hazards are specific and well understood: activation outliers in certain channels, sensitivity of attention and normalisation layers, and degradation that appears on long-context or multilingual inputs but not on short English benchmarks.把权重量化到 FP8、INT8 或 4 位，既减少了每 token 需要读取的字节数，也减少了装下模型所需的 HBM。由于解码受带宽限制，把权重字节数减半，几乎就等于把解码时延减半。风险是具体且已被充分认识的：某些通道上的激活离群值、注意力层与归一化层的敏感性，以及那种只在长上下文或多语言输入上出现、在短英文基准上却看不出来的性能退化。

The KV cache can be quantised too, often to 8 or even 4 bits, which directly buys concurrent users. Sound practice: quantise, then evaluate on your traffic — not on the benchmark suite.KV 缓存同样可以量化，常见到 8 位甚至 4 位，这会直接换来更多并发用户。稳妥的做法是：先量化，然后在你自己的真实流量上评测——而不是在基准测试集上。

Disaggregated serving recognises that prefill and decode want different hardware. Run them on separate pools — prefill on compute-dense nodes, decode on bandwidth-and-capacity-dense ones — and ship the KV cache between them over the fabric. This removes the interference where a long prefill stalls everyone's token stream, and lets each pool be sized and scaled independently.分离式服务承认预填充与解码想要的是不同的硬件。把它们跑在两个独立的资源池上——预填充放在算力密集的节点上，解码放在带宽与容量密集的节点上——再通过网络把 KV 缓存在两者之间搬运。这消除了“一次长预填充卡住所有人的出词流”的相互干扰，也让两个池可以各自独立地定容和扩缩。

For mixture-of-experts models, add expert parallelism across the scale-up domain, plus routing-aware batching so that tokens headed for the same experts are grouped. The upper bound on serving efficiency for a large MoE is set almost entirely by how well this is done.对混合专家模型，还要在纵向扩展域内加上专家并行，并配合感知路由的批处理，把去往同一批专家的 token 聚在一起。一个大型 MoE 的服务效率上限，几乎完全取决于这件事做得有多好。

The reasoning-era correction推理模型时代的修正

Per-token prices have fallen dramatically and continue to. Cost per completed task has not fallen nearly as fast, because reasoning models and agentic loops consume vastly more tokens per task — internal chains of thought, multiple tool calls, retries, and re-sent context on every step. Both statements are true simultaneously. When someone cites falling token prices as evidence that AI is becoming cheap, ask for cost per completed task instead.每 token 价格已经大幅下降，并且还在继续下降。但每完成一个任务的成本，下降得远没有那么快，因为推理模型和智能体循环在每个任务上消耗的 token 多得多——内部思维链、多次工具调用、重试，以及每一步都要重发的上下文。这两句话同时成立。当有人拿 token 价格下降来论证 AI 正在变便宜时，请改问每完成一个任务的成本。

13.2  How to read a model release, in six questions13.2 用六个问题读懂一次模型发布

  1. What are the active and total parameters?激活参数和总参数各是多少？

    For a mixture-of-experts model these differ by an order of magnitude. Active parameters predict speed and FLOP cost; total parameters predict the HBM you must own to serve it at all.对混合专家模型来说，这两个数字相差一个数量级。激活参数预示速度和 FLOP 成本；总参数预示你要想跑起来必须拥有多少 HBM。

  2. What is the context window, and what is the useful context window?上下文窗口是多少，而真正可用的上下文窗口又是多少？

    Advertised maximum length and the length at which retrieval accuracy holds up are different numbers. Look for evaluations that place information at varied depths in a long input, not just a stated maximum.标称的最大长度，和检索准确率仍然站得住的长度，是两个不同的数字。要看那种把信息放在长输入不同深度处的评测，而不只是一个标称上限。

  3. Which modalities, in and out?支持哪些模态，输入还是输出？

    Text, images, audio, video, and whether each is input-only or also output. Native multimodality — trained jointly rather than bolted together from separate models — behaves noticeably differently on tasks that mix modalities.文本、图像、音频、视频，以及每一种是仅输入还是也能输出。原生多模态——联合训练出来的，而不是把几个独立模型拼起来的——在跨模态混合任务上的表现明显不同。

  4. Is it a reasoning model, and is the budget controllable?它是不是推理模型？思考预算能不能调？

    Models that emit an extended internal chain before answering trade latency and token cost for accuracy on hard problems. What matters commercially is whether you can dial that budget per request — one setting for a cheap classification, another for a difficult proof.会在回答前吐出一长串内部思维链的模型，是用时延和 token 成本去换难题上的准确率。商业上真正重要的是，你能不能按请求调节这个预算——一个廉价分类用一档，一道困难证明用另一档。

  5. How good is tool use, specifically?工具使用具体做得怎么样？

    For anything agentic this dominates. Reliable structured output, correct parallel tool calls, sensible recovery from a tool error, and knowing when to stop. It is poorly captured by general benchmarks and best measured on your own task.对任何智能体场景，这一项都占主导。可靠的结构化输出、正确的并行工具调用、在工具报错后合理地恢复，以及知道什么时候该停。通用基准很难反映它，最好在你自己的任务上测。

  6. What is the deprecation and versioning policy?弃用与版本策略是什么？

    A model that silently changes behaviour under a stable name will break your evaluations and your product. Pinned versions and published sunset dates are a real procurement criterion, not a formality.一个在同一个名字下悄悄改变行为的模型，会毁掉你的评测和你的产品。可锁定的版本号和公开的下线日期，是实打实的采购标准，不是形式。

Benchmarks: use them, do not trust them基准测试：可以用，但别信

Public benchmarks suffer from saturation (everyone is near the ceiling, so differences are noise), contamination (test items leak into training corpora), and overfitting (targets that are optimised for stop measuring what they proxied). They remain useful as a floor — a model that fails them is genuinely weak — and nearly useless as a ranking near the top. The only evaluation that reliably predicts your outcome is one built from your own traffic, with your own graders, versioned like code.公开基准存在三个问题：饱和（大家都逼近天花板，差异变成噪声）、污染（测试题渗进了训练语料），以及过拟合（一旦某个目标被专门优化，它就不再衡量原本要代表的东西）。它们作为下限仍然有用——过不了基准的模型确实弱——但在顶部作为排名几乎毫无价值。唯一能可靠预测你实际结果的评测，是用你自己的流量、你自己的评分器构建，并像代码一样做版本管理的那一套。

14.1  The agent loop, and where each step goes wrong14.1 智能体循环，以及每一步会怎样出错

  1. Assemble context组装上下文

    System instructions, task, relevant retrieved material, tool schemas, and conversation history.     A tool runs — a search, a database query, code execution in a sandbox, a write to a real system. This is the point at which the system stops being a chatbot and acquires the ability to cause consequences.一个工具跑起来——一次搜索、一次数据库查询、在沙箱里执行一段代码，或者向一个真实系统写入。正是在这个点上，系统不再是一个聊天机器人，而获得了造成后果的能力。

    failure mode: unbounded side effects; no dry-run; no idempotency失效模式：副作用没有边界；没有试运行；没有幂等性

  4. Observe观察

    Results come back, possibly large, possibly an error. Truncation and summarisation policy here quietly determines whether the agent can recover from failure or spirals.结果返回，可能很大，也可能是一个错误。这里的截断与摘要策略，悄悄决定了智能体是能从失败中恢复，还是就此陷入死循环。

    Loop until done. Knowing when to stop is an underrated capability — both stopping early with a partial answer and looping forever are common, expensive failures.循环直到完成。知道什么时候该停是一项被低估的能力——过早停下给出半成品，和永远停不下来，都是常见且昂贵的失败。

Why long-horizon autonomy is hard为什么长链路自主如此困难
P(task succeeds) ≈ pn   for n independent steps at per-step reliability p

At p = 0.95: ten steps gives 60%, fifty steps gives 8%. This is why the practical frontier of agents is measured in task horizon — how long a task an agent can complete unattended — and why the engineering answers are verification, checkpointing, and recovery rather than better prompts. Real systems beat the naive exponential by making steps checkable and retryable, so failures do not compound.当 p = 0.95 时：十步剩 60%，五十步只剩 8%。这就是为什么智能体的实际前沿是用任务时长来衡量的——一个智能体能在无人看管下完成多长的任务——也是为什么工程上的答案是验证、检查点和恢复，而不是更好的提示词。真实系统之所以能跑赢这条朴素指数曲线，靠的是让每一步都可校验、可重试，从而不让失败复合。

The clean way to think about margin in this chain is not "who is closest to the customer" but "who has no substitute right now". Every layer with a single credible supplier prices like a monopoly; every layer with five interchangeable suppliers prices like a commodity. Because the binding constraint moves (§00), so does the margin — and it can move much faster than the physical assets can.思考这条链上利润归属的干净方式，不是“谁离客户最近”，而是“此刻谁没有替代品”。任何只有一家可信供应商的层级，定价方式都像垄断；任何有五家可互换供应商的层级，定价方式都像大宗商品。由于紧约束是会移动的（§00），利润也会随之移动——而且它移动的速度，可以远快于实物资产。

16.1  The depreciation argument, stated fairly16.1 公平地陈述折旧之争

The most consequential open question in AI accounting is how long an accelerator remains economically productive. Depreciation schedules in the range of five to six years are common. Both sides of the argument are serious:AI 会计中最具影响力的一个未决问题是：一张加速器在经济上还能高产多久。五到六年的折旧年限比较常见。争论的双方都很严肃：

The case for longer useful life支持使用寿命更长的理由The case for shorter支持使用寿命更短的理由
Older accelerators cascade down to inference, batch jobs, fine-tuning and research once they leave frontier training.旧加速器在退出前沿训练之后，会向下梯次转用于推理、批处理作业、微调和研究。Each generation improves performance per watt substantially. Old hardware is displaced by operating cost, not by failure.每一代的每瓦性能都有实质提升。淘汰旧硬件的是运营成本，不是故障。
Demand has consistently exceeded supply, so nothing has yet had to be retired early.需求持续超过供给，因此至今还没有什么东西不得不提前退役。Power and floor space are the scarce inputs. A less efficient part occupying constrained megawatts has a real opportunity cost.电力与场地才是稀缺投入。一颗效率更低的芯片占着受限的兆瓦，机会成本是真实的。
Physical failure rates over five years are manageable and well understood.五年期内的物理故障率是可控的，而且已被充分了解。Memory capacity per accelerator is rising fast; parts that cannot hold current models are functionally obsolete regardless of their FLOPs.每张加速器的显存容量在快速上升；装不下当前模型的芯片，无论 FLOPs 多少，在功能上都已过时。
Software improvements keep extracting more from installed hardware.软件改进持续从已装机的硬件上榨出更多性能。Those same improvements land on new hardware too — and usually work better there.同样这些改进也会落到新硬件上——而且通常在新硬件上效果更好。

The resolution is probably that useful life is real but conditional: long while demand outruns supply and power is the constraint on adding capacity; much shorter the moment demand growth slows, because then the cheapest way to serve a token is on the newest silicon and the old fleet has nowhere to go. That is a genuine risk, and it is not distributed evenly — it falls hardest on operators whose entire balance sheet is one hardware generation.答案大概是：使用寿命是真实的，但有条件——在需求跑赢供给、且电力是新增产能约束的时期，它很长；而一旦需求增速放缓，它就会大幅缩短，因为那时服务一个 token 最便宜的方式就是用最新的硅，旧机队将无处可去。这是一个真实的风险，而且分布并不均匀——它对那些整张资产负债表都押在同一代硬件上的运营商打击最重。

16.2  Three questions that cut through most AI financial commentary16.2 三个能穿透大多数 AI 财务评论的问题

Is the revenue recurring or is it a build-out?这笔收入是经常性的，还是一次性建设带来的？
the composition question构成问题

Selling infrastructure into a construction boom and selling a service to end users look identical on a revenue line and behave completely differently when the boom pauses.在一轮建设热潮中卖基础设施，和向最终用户卖服务，在收入这一行上看起来完全一样，但当热潮暂停时，两者的表现截然不同。

Are the counterparties independent?交易对手是彼此独立的吗？
the circularity question循环性问题

When a supplier invests in a customer who then buys from the supplier, revenue is real but it is not independent demand. Map the arrows before drawing conclusions from the totals.当一家供应商投资了一个客户、而这个客户又回过头来向它采购时，收入是真实的，但它不是独立需求。在从总额得出结论之前，先把箭头的方向画清楚。

What is the cost per completed task, over time?每完成一个任务的成本，随时间如何变化？
the unit-economics question单位经济问题

Not cost per token, and not gross margin on an API call. Cost per unit of work the customer actually values — including retries, failed runs and human review.不是每 token 成本，也不是一次 API 调用的毛利率，而是客户真正看重的那一单位工作的成本——把重试、失败运行和人工复核都算进去。

18.2  A checklist for any AI claim18.2 面对任何 AI 论断的检查清单

  1. Which layer is this about?这说的是哪一层？

    A packaging story is not a model story. A model story is not a revenue story. Most confused analysis is a layer error.一条封装新闻不是一条模型新闻。一条模型新闻不是一条收入新闻。大多数混乱的分析都源于层级错位。

  2. Which clock does it run on?它跑在哪只时钟上？

    Software improvements land in weeks. Fabs and substations land in years. Predictions that mix the two produce nonsense in both directions.软件改进以周为单位落地。晶圆厂和变电站以年为单位落地。把两者混在一起的预测，在两个方向上都会得出胡话。

  3. Does it move the binding constraint or a slack one?它移动的是紧约束，还是一个有余量的环节？

    A 2× improvement in something that is not the bottleneck produces a 0% improvement in outcomes.在不是瓶颈的地方做 2 倍改进，对结果的改善是 0%。

  4. Is the number normalised?这个数字归一化了吗？

    FLOPs: which precision, dense or sparse, per die or per package? Tokens: input or output, cached or not? Megawatts: IT load or facility load? Most disagreements dissolve on normalisation.FLOPs：哪种精度？稠密还是稀疏？按裸片还是按封装？Token：输入还是输出？是否命中缓存？兆瓦：IT 负荷还是设施总负荷？大多数分歧在归一化之后就消失了。

  5. What would falsify it?什么能证伪它？

    If the claim survives every possible observation, it is a mood, not an analysis. Write down in advance what you would expect to see if it were wrong.如果一条论断在任何可能的观测下都能成立，那它是一种情绪，不是一份分析。请事先写下：如果它是错的，你预期会看到什么。

TSVTSV
through-silicon via硅通孔

A vertical copper connection drilled through a thinned die. What makes stacked memory possible.打穿一颗减薄裸片的垂直铜连接。堆叠内存之所以可能，靠的就是它。

Hybrid bonding混合键合

HBMHBM

Stacked DRAM with a very wide interface, mounted beside the compute die. 1024-bit per stack at HBM3E, 2048-bit at HBM4.接口极宽的堆叠 DRAM，安装在计算裸片旁边。HBM3E 每堆栈 1024 位，HBM4 为 2048 位。

NVLink / UALink / ICINVLink / UALink / ICI
scale-up interconnects纵向扩展互连

Very high bandwidth, memory-semantic links inside an accelerator domain. Distinct from the packet-switched scale-out network.加速器域内部的超高带宽、内存语义链路。与分组交换的横向扩展网络不同。

RoCERoCE

LPO / CPOLPO / CPO

Removing the DSP from a transceiver, or moving the optical engine onto the switch package. Both target watts per port at scale.从收发模块中去掉 DSP，或者把光引擎搬到交换芯片封装上。两者的目标都是大规模下每端口的瓦数。

PUEPUE

Facility power ÷ IT power. 1.1–1.3 for a well-run liquid-cooled AI site. Measures only facility overhead, not useful work.设施功率 ÷ IT 功率。运营良好的液冷 AI 站点为 1.1–1.3。它只衡量设施开销，不衡量有用功。

Direct-to-chip liquid cooling芯片直接液冷
DLCDLC

Cold plates on the hot components with a pumped coolant loop. The current default above roughly 50 kW per rack.在发热部件上安装冷板，配泵驱冷却液回路。在每机柜约 50 kW 以上，这是当前的默认方案。

Interconnection queue并网排队
grid connection电网接入

The multi-year utility process for connecting large new load. The single slowest step in the entire chain.电力公司接入大型新增负荷的多年期流程。整条链上最慢的一步。

TokenToken（词元）
unit of text文本的计量单位

A sub-word chunk produced by the tokeniser. The unit of pricing, context length and throughput.分词器产生的一个子词片段。计价、上下文长度和吞吐的计量单位。

BPEBPE
byte-pair encoding字节对编码

The usual tokenisation algorithm. Vocabulary size trades table memory against sequence compression, especially for non-English text and code.常用的分词算法。词表大小是在词表内存与序列压缩率之间做权衡，对非英语文本和代码尤其明显。

Arithmetic intensity算术强度
FLOP per byte每字节 FLOP 数

Determines whether a kernel is compute-bound or memory-bound. The most load-bearing concept in AI systems performance.决定一个算子是算力受限还是内存受限。AI 系统性能中最吃重的一个概念。

Roofline屋顶线
performance model性能模型

A plot bounding attainable performance by bandwidth on one side and peak compute on the other.一张图，一侧用带宽、另一侧用峰值算力，共同框定可达性能的上界。

MFUMFU
model FLOPs utilisation模型算力利用率

Useful model FLOPs ÷ peak hardware FLOPs over real wall-clock. The honest efficiency number.在真实墙钟时间内，有效模型 FLOP ÷ 硬件峰值 FLOP。诚实的效率数字。

Goodput有效吞吐（Goodput）
effective throughput真实吞吐

Work that survives failures and restarts. Diverges sharply from raw throughput at cluster scale.在故障与重启之后仍然存活的工作量。在集群规模上会与原始吞吐急剧分离。

Data / tensor / pipeline parallelism数据 / 张量 / 流水线并行
DP / TP / PPDP / TP / PP

Split the batch, split each layer's matrices, or split the layers across devices. Composed, not chosen.切批、切每层的矩阵，或者把层切到不同设备上。这三者是组合使用，而非二选一。

Expert parallelism专家并行
EPEP

Distribute mixture-of-experts experts across devices and route tokens to them. Requires all-to-all communication.把混合专家的专家分布到各设备上，并把 token 路由过去。需要 all-to-all 通信。

ZeRO / FSDPZeRO / FSDP
sharded data parallelism切分式数据并行

Shard optimiser state, gradients and parameters across data-parallel ranks; gather just in time. Cuts the 16-bytes-per-parameter bill.把优化器状态、梯度和参数在数据并行进程间切分，用时再聚合。削掉那份“每参数 16 字节”的账单。

Activation checkpointing激活值检查点
recomputation重算

Discard intermediate activations and recompute them in the backward pass. Trades roughly 30% more compute for a large memory saving.丢弃中间激活值，在反向传播时重新算出来。用大约多 30% 的算力，换取显存上的大幅节省。

Pipeline bubble流水线气泡
idle time空闲时间

The fill and drain periods of a pipeline schedule: (P−1)/(m+P−1) of the step.流水线调度中的填充与排空阶段：占一步的 (P−1)/(m+P−1)。

Scaling law缩放定律
empirical regularity经验规律

Predictable improvement in loss as parameters, data and compute grow together. Used to plan runs before committing to them.当参数、数据与算力同步增长时，loss 呈可预测的改善。用于在投入之前规划训练。

Chinchilla-optimalChinchilla 最优
≈20 tokens/parameter约每参数 20 个 token

Loss-minimising allocation for a fixed training budget. Deliberately violated in practice to get cheaper inference.给定训练预算下最小化 loss 的配比。实践中会被刻意违背，以换取更便宜的推理。

MoEMoE
mixture of experts混合专家

Many parallel expert sub-networks with a router activating a few per token. High total capacity, low per-token compute, high memory requirement.许多并行的专家子网络，配一个路由器，每个 token 只激活其中少数几个。总容量高、每 token 算力低、显存需求高。

SFTSFT
supervised fine-tuning监督微调

Training on curated demonstrations. Turns a text completer into an instruction follower.在精心整理的示范上训练。把一个文本续写器变成一个指令遵循者。

RLHF / DPORLHF / DPO
preference optimisation偏好优化

Learning from human comparisons — via an explicit reward model and RL, or directly from preference pairs.从人类比较中学习——通过显式奖励模型加强化学习，或直接从偏好对中学习。

RLVRRLVR
RL with verifiable rewards可验证奖励的强化学习

Reinforcement learning where correctness is checked automatically. The main driver of recent reasoning gains.正确性可被自动核验的强化学习。近期推理能力提升的主要驱动力。

Distillation蒸馏
teacher → student教师 → 学生

Training a small model on a large one's outputs. How frontier capability becomes economically servable.用大模型的输出训练一个小模型。前沿能力如何变成经济上可服务的东西。

Prefill / decode预填充 / 解码
inference phases推理的两个阶段

Process the prompt in parallel (compute-bound), then generate tokens one at a time (bandwidth-bound).先并行处理提示词（算力受限），再一次一个地生成 token（带宽受限）。

KV cacheKV 缓存
attention state注意力状态

Cached keys and values for every previous token. Grows linearly with context and concurrency; usually the real capacity limit in serving.为此前每一个 token 缓存的 key 与 value。随上下文长度与并发数线性增长；通常才是推理服务中真正的容量上限。

GQA / MQAGQA / MQA
grouped / multi-query attention分组 / 多查询注意力

Share key-value heads across query heads. Shrinks the KV cache by a large factor and makes long context affordable.让多个查询头共享 key-value 头。把 KV 缓存缩小若干倍，使长上下文变得负担得起。

Paged attention分页注意力
KV memory managementKV 显存管理

Block-allocated KV cache with an indirection table. Eliminates fragmentation and enables prefix sharing.用间接映射表管理的块式 KV 缓存。消除碎片，并支持前缀共享。

Continuous batching连续批处理
in-flight batchingin-flight batching

Admit and retire sequences at every decode step so the batch never idles. The largest single serving-throughput win.在每一步解码时放入新序列、淘汰完成的序列，让这一批永不空转。推理吞吐上收益最大的单项优化。

Speculative decoding投机解码
draft and verify起草并验证

A cheap drafter proposes several tokens; the full model verifies them in one pass. Faster, with an unchanged output distribution.一个廉价的起草者先提出若干 token；完整模型用一次前向把它们全部验证。更快，且输出分布不变。

Disaggregated serving分离式服务
split prefill/decode预填充与解码分离

Run the two inference phases on separately sized hardware pools and transfer the KV cache between them.把推理的两个阶段跑在各自定容的硬件池上，并在两者之间传输 KV 缓存。

TTFT / TPOTTTFT / TPOT
latency metrics时延指标

Time to first token, and time per output token thereafter. The two numbers users actually feel.首 token 时延，以及之后每个输出 token 的耗时。用户真正能感受到的两个数字。

Quantisation量化
reduced precision降低精度

Storing weights or activations in fewer bits. Directly buys decode speed, since decode is bandwidth-bound.用更少的位数存储权重或激活值。由于解码受带宽限制，这直接换来解码速度。

Foundation model基础模型
general pre-trained model通用预训练模型

Broadly trained, adapted to many tasks rather than built for one.经过广泛训练，可适配到许多任务，而不是为某一个任务而造。

Open weights开放权重
≠ open source≠ 开源

Weights are downloadable, usually under a bespoke licence, usually without the training data. Read the licence.权重可下载，通常附带一份自定义许可证，通常不含训练数据。请读许可证。

Context window上下文窗口
input length limit输入长度上限

Maximum tokens the model can attend to. The useful window — where accuracy holds — is often shorter than the advertised one.模型能够注意到的最大 token 数。真正可用的窗口——准确率仍然站得住的那一段——往往短于标称值。

Reasoning model推理模型
test-time compute推理时算力

Emits an extended internal chain before answering. Trades latency and tokens for accuracy on hard problems.在回答前先吐出一长串内部思维链。用时延和 token 换取难题上的准确率。

Multimodal多模态
beyond text超越文本

Handles images, audio or video. Native joint training behaves differently from separate models stitched together.能处理图像、音频或视频。原生联合训练的表现，与把几个独立模型拼起来明显不同。

Agent智能体（Agent）
model in a loop循环中的模型

A model with tools, state and the ability to take multi-step actions toward a goal.一个带工具、带状态、能朝目标采取多步行动的模型。

Tool use / function calling工具使用 / 函数调用
structured actions结构化动作

The model emits a structured call against a declared schema; the platform executes it and returns the result.模型按声明好的 schema 发出一次结构化调用；平台执行它并返回结果。

MCPMCP
Model Context ProtocolModel Context Protocol

An open protocol for exposing tools and data sources to models, widely adopted across vendors.一个把工具与数据源暴露给模型的开放协议，已被多家厂商广泛采用。

RAGRAG
retrieval-augmented generation检索增强生成

Fetch relevant material and place it in context. Hybrid retrieval with reranking beats pure vector search in most production settings.取回相关材料并放进上下文。在大多数生产场景下，混合检索加重排优于纯向量检索。

Prompt injection提示注入
the structural vulnerability结构性漏洞

Instructions hidden in content the model reads. Mitigated architecturally — least privilege, confirmation, isolation — not by prompting.藏在模型所读内容中的指令。只能靠架构缓解——最小权限、人工确认、隔离——而不是靠提示词。

Hallucination幻觉
confident error自信的错误

Fluent, plausible, wrong. Reduced by grounding and verification; not eliminated by either.流畅、看似合理、但是错的。可以靠事实接地与验证减少；但两者都无法消除它。

Task horizon任务时长
autonomy metric自主性指标

How long a task a system can complete unattended at a given reliability. More informative than any capability benchmark.在给定可靠性下，系统能无人看管完成多长的任务。比任何能力基准都更有信息量。

Eval评测集（Eval）
evaluation suite评测套件

A versioned dataset plus graders. The only reliable predictor of whether a change helped your product.一个带版本的数据集加上评分器。唯一能可靠预测一次改动是否有益于你产品的东西。

Guardrails护栏
policy enforcement策略执行

Input and output checks around the model. Necessary, insufficient alone, and best combined with limits on what the system can do.围绕模型的输入与输出检查。必要但不充分，最好与“限制系统能做什么”配合使用。

FLOP vs FLOP/sFLOP 与 FLOP/s
work vs rate工作量与速率

A count of operations versus operations per second. Conflating them is the most common unit error in AI writing.一个是运算次数的计数，一个是每秒运算次数。混淆两者是 AI 写作中最常见的单位错误。

Accelerator-hour加速器小时
the compute unit算力单位

One accelerator for one hour. Compare only after normalising for generation, precision and utilisation.一张加速器运行一小时。只有在按代际、精度和利用率归一化之后才能比较。

Capex vs opex资本开支与运营开支
build vs run建与运

Buying hardware and buildings versus paying for power, staff and rented capacity. AI shifts spend toward capex and then toward its depreciation.购买硬件和建筑，对比支付电费、人力和租用产能。AI 把支出推向资本开支，随后又推向它的折旧。

Cost per completed task每完成一个任务的成本
the honest metric诚实的指标

Total cost of an outcome, including retries, tool calls and human review. Falling far more slowly than cost per token.一个结果的总成本，含重试、工具调用与人工复核。它下降的速度远慢于每 token 成本。

Utilisation利用率
fleet efficiency机队效率

Fraction of purchased capacity doing paid work. Diurnal demand makes serving utilisation structurally lower than training utilisation.已购产能中真正在做付费工作的比例。昼夜需求波动使推理服务的利用率在结构上低于训练。

Take-or-pay / reserved capacity照付不议 / 预留产能
committed spend承诺消费额

Guaranteed access to accelerators for a fixed term. Secures supply; concentrates risk if plans change.在固定期限内保证获得加速器。锁定了供应；也在计划变化时集中了风险。

Circular revenue循环收入
related-party demand关联方需求

A supplier funds a customer who buys from the supplier. Real revenue, but not independent evidence of demand.供应商出资给客户，客户再向供应商采购。收入是真实的，但它不是独立需求的证据。
