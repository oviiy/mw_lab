# Pre-publication notes

## 1. What changed after the independent review

| Issue raised | Change made |
|---|---|
| "Delayed" loss recognition not shown | Title changed to **Gradual** Loss Recognition. Added a lead–lag test and an ex-post test (new Section 4.6, Table 2). Neither the higher nor the lower mark is systematically right (15 vs 17), and 24 of 40 gaps of 5+ points never closed. |
| Event study could only see slow declines (par-loan filter) | Event study and transitions now use **all term debt**. Debt-for-equity exchanges are detected (new equity while debt shrinks or is impaired). Abrupt cases are counted: 31 abrupt vs 134 gradual (about 19%). |
| Inconsistent denominators (56% vs 14%) | All event-time shares now use the same 81 events with a full year of history (47% / 9%). |
| Restructured loans counted as "recovered" | Post-restructuring paper (e.g. Pluralsight) is no longer read as a recovery to par. |
| Thin samples behind named lenders | Figure 4 now shows 95% intervals clustered by borrower, plus borrower counts. Lenders with fewer than 10 borrowers are greyed out and not ranked. Medians are reported in the text. |
| Ceiling effect near par | Gap ÷ implied discount added (Table 2): 27–39% below a mark of 97. |
| Is dispersion rising? | Year-by-year trend on all lenders and on a fixed panel (Table 2), reported with caveats. |
| The "so what" in dollars | Dollar effect by lender (Table 2). It is small: Prospect +$21m, or 0.42% of par-loan principal; Ares −$29m, or −0.13%. |
| Exits | Bounds for the 60–70 bucket (all exits repaid vs none). |
| PIK claim | Wilson 95% intervals added; the difference is described as suggestive. The noisy trend and growth of the loan book are disclosed. |
| Factual wording | Fixed: "80 = one-fifth loss"; "NAV = sum of marks"; the legal conclusion on "permissible"; the unsourced $1.7tn; the unsupported claim that large lenders know more. |
| Help/Systems verification | Checked against Prospect's 10-Q: second lien, 12.74% (SOFR + 9.00% PIK), maturing 19 May 2029, $56.3m principal. Palmer Square's line: same tranche and spread, maturing 21 May 2029, $3.9m. |
