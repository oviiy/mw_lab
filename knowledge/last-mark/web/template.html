    Abstract摘要

    Most private loans have no market price, so the value investors see is the lender's own estimate. Business development companies (BDCs) publish that estimate for every loan each quarter, and large loans are often held by several of them at once. Using schedules of investments from BDCs (2022–2026) that reconcile to their reported totals, I identify loan-quarters in which two or more BDCs hold the same facility ( borrowers). Four results emerge. First, marks on performing loans are nearly identical across lenders (median gap ), while on loans marked below 85 the median gap is (n = ) and the widest single case is points. Second, most losses are recognized gradually: among loans first marked below 60 with a full year of history, were at 90 or above four quarters earlier and one quarter earlier, although about one case in five moves from 90+ to distress or restructuring within a single quarter. Third, disagreement is not simply one lender lagging another: neither the higher nor the lower mark is systematically closer to where the loan stood a year later, and many gaps never close. Fourth, the dollar effect on any one fund is small, a few tenths of a percent of its loan book, so the issue is the timing of the signal investors receive rather than inflated net asset values.

    Main results主要结果

      1Introduction引言

      Private credit has become one of the largest sources of financing for mid-sized companies, much of it lent to sponsor-owned firms through bilateral or club deals that rarely trade. With no secondary-market price, the value at which these loans are carried is a model-based estimate (a "Level 3" fair value in accounting terms). These estimates set reported net asset values; at most externally managed BDCs they also set the base management fee, which is charged on gross assets; and they are the main measure of credit losses most investors see. That audience is widening as non-traded BDCs are sold to wealth-management clients.

      私募信贷已成为中型企业最主要的融资来源之一，其中大部分通过双边或俱乐部贷款借给由私募股权控股的企业，这些贷款很少交易。由于没有二级市场价格，贷款的账面价值是基于模型的估计（会计上称为"第三层级"公允价值）。这些估计决定了基金申报的净值；在大多数外部管理的 BDC 中，基础管理费按总资产收取，因此也取决于这些估计；它们还是多数投资者能看到的主要信用损失度量。随着非上市 BDC 向财富管理客户销售，这一受众正在扩大。

      A recurring concern is that such marks are slow to reflect bad news: loans are said to remain "at par until default." Testing the concern is difficult because the true value of an illiquid loan is unobserved. This paper uses a natural benchmark. When several BDCs hold the same loan, they are valuing an identical claim on the same date, under the same accounting standard. Differences between their marks therefore isolate valuation judgment from differences in the underlying asset.

      一个反复出现的担忧是，这类估值对坏消息反应迟缓，即所谓"违约前一直按面值计价"。由于流动性差的贷款真实价值不可观测，这一担忧难以检验。本文利用一个天然的基准：当多家 BDC 持有同一笔贷款时，它们是在同一日期、同一会计准则下，为完全相同的债权估值。因此，它们之间的估值差异可以把估值判断与资产本身的差异区分开来。

      The paper builds an open, loan-level panel of BDC marks directly from inline XBRL filings, admitting a filing only if its line items sum to the portfolio total the company reports. It then asks four questions: how much lenders disagree about the same loan, and how that varies with credit quality; how marks evolve before a loan becomes distressed; whether the lender that marks lower turns out to be right; and what a given mark implies a year later. Industry analysts have reported cross-lender dispersion using proprietary data (PIMCO, 2026) and normalized non-accruals across lenders (Octus, 2026). What is new here is that the analysis uses public filings only, matches individual facilities, and links every observation to its source document so that any figure can be checked.

      本文直接从 inline XBRL 申报文件构建公开的、逐笔贷款层面的 BDC 估值面板，只纳入逐笔加总与公司自报组合总额一致的文件。在此基础上回答四个问题：各机构对同一笔贷款的分歧有多大，如何随信用质量变化；贷款陷入困境前估值如何演变；估值较低的机构事后是否更正确；某一估值水平对一年后意味着什么。业界分析曾利用付费数据报告机构间估值分歧（PIMCO，2026），并在机构间对齐不良贷款（Octus，2026）。本文的新意在于：只用公开申报、逐笔匹配贷款，并将每个观测链接到原始文件，任何数字都可核查。

      2Institutional background制度背景

      BDCs are closed-end investment companies, many of them exchange-listed, that lend primarily to private middle-market firms. Under the Investment Company Act they must report their investments at fair value each quarter; since 2022 Rule 2a-5 has governed how boards (or a designated valuation adviser) determine those values. Each 10-Q and 10-K contains a schedule of investments listing every position with its principal, amortized cost and fair value, and these schedules are tagged in inline XBRL.

      BDC 是封闭式投资公司，许多在交易所上市，主要向未上市的中型企业放贷。根据《投资公司法》，BDC 须每季度按公允价值申报其投资；自 2022 年起，Rule 2a-5 规范了董事会（或指定估值顾问）确定公允价值的方式。每份 10-Q 和 10-K 都包含一张持仓明细表（schedule of investments），逐笔列出本金、摊余成本和公允价值，并以 inline XBRL 标注。

      Throughout, a loan's mark is its fair value divided by its principal, in cents per dollar. A mark of 100 means the lender values the loan at par. A mark of 80 means it values it at 80 cents, which reflects both the losses it expects and the higher return a buyer would now demand for the risk; it is not a pure loss forecast. Box 1 shows the scale used in the figures.

      本文中，贷款的估值（mark）定义为公允价值除以本金，以每 1 美元本金对应的美分表示。100 表示按面值计价；80 表示按 80 美分计价，它既反映预期损失，也反映买方现在对该风险要求的更高回报，并非单纯的损失预测。框 1 给出图中使用的刻度。

      3Data and sample construction数据与样本构建

      3.1Source and reconciliation数据来源与核对

      I download the XBRL instance of every 10-Q and 10-K filed by 28 BDCs from mid-2022 through and retain facts dated at the period end that carry an investment identifier: fair value, amortized cost, principal, interest rate and PIK rate. Filers differ in how they tag subtotals, affiliated positions and joint ventures. Each filing must therefore pass a reconciliation test before use: the sum of line-item fair values must lie within 3% of the total investments at fair value that the company itself reports. lender-quarters pass; the remainder are excluded (Appendix Figure A1). Ten BDCs, including FS KKR and Oaktree Specialty Lending, never pass, chiefly because of joint-venture tagging.

      本文下载了 28 家 BDC 自 2022 年中至 提交的每一份 10-Q 和 10-K 的 XBRL 实例文件，保留期末日期、带有投资标识的事实：公允价值、摊余成本、本金、利率和 PIK 利率。各申报方在小计、关联方持仓和合资企业的标注方式上存在差异，因此每份文件在使用前须通过核对检验：逐笔公允价值之和须与公司自报的投资总额相差不超过 3%。共有 个机构-季度通过检验，其余剔除（见附录图 A1）。包括 FS KKR 和 Oaktree Specialty Lending 在内的 10 家 BDC 从未通过，主要原因是合资企业持仓的标注方式。

      3.2Identifying the same loan识别同一笔贷款

      The unit of analysis is a lender's exposure to a borrower in a quarter. Two holdings are treated as the same loan only if the borrower, the interest rate (to within 0.15 percentage points) and the seniority all match, and the match recurs in an adjacent quarter. The repeat test matters because most of these loans pay a floating rate that resets with SOFR, the benchmark rate: two different loans may share a coupon by coincidence once, but rarely twice. Revolving credit lines and delayed-draw tranches, whose reported principal can include undrawn amounts, are excluded, as are split "first-out/last-out" structures, in which one first-lien loan is divided into senior and junior pieces. The comparison uses par loans only: lines carried at an amortized cost of 90–105% of principal, which removes discounted purchases and restructured paper.

      分析单位为某机构在某季度对某借款人的敞口。只有当借款人、利率（误差不超过 0.15 个百分点）和清偿顺位都一致，且在相邻季度再次匹配时，两笔持仓才被视为同一笔贷款。重复匹配很关键：这类贷款大多按随基准利率 SOFR 重置的浮动利率计息，两笔不同的贷款可能偶然一次利率相同，却很难连续两次相同。循环信贷额度与延迟提款额度（其申报本金可能含未提款部分）以及"first-out/last-out"分层结构（一笔一留置权贷款被拆成优先和次级两部分）均被排除。比较只使用正常贷款：摊余成本为本金 90%–105% 的持仓，以剔除折价买入和重组后的债务。

      4.6Who was right?谁更接近事实？

      If the higher-marking lender were simply slow, the gap would close over time with that lender moving down, and the lower mark would prove the better forecast. Neither happens consistently. Taking the first disagreement of at least three points on each stressed shared loan and comparing both marks with where the same lenders stood four quarters later, the lower mark was closer in cases and the higher mark in (with restructured and exited). Following each pair of lenders through time from the first quarter in which their marks diverged by five points or more, of gaps had not closed to within two points by the end of the sample.       5Robustness and limitations稳健性与局限

      Matching error. Participants in a club deal report the same coupon because it belongs to the facility, and requiring a repeat match in an adjacent quarter guards against coincidental coupon equality. Several candidate matches with very large gaps were inspected against the filings and excluded because they combined different tranches (for example, a split first-out/last-out structure or a largely undrawn delayed-draw line); the rules in Section 3.2 now exclude these categories. Residual mismatches are possible, so individual observations should be checked against the linked filings.

      匹配误差。俱乐部贷款的参与方报告相同的利率，因为利率属于贷款本身；要求相邻季度再次匹配，可防止利率偶然相同造成的误配。本文对若干差距极大的候选匹配逐一对照原始申报，发现其混合了不同档次（如 first-out/last-out 分层结构，或基本未提款的延迟提款额度），因而剔除；3.2 节的规则已排除这些类别。仍可能存在少量误配，单个观测应对照所附原始文件核实。

      Information differences. Lenders may hold different information about a borrower, for example if one is the agent or sits on a lender committee. The filings do not record these roles. The marks compared are nonetheless as of the same date, and a persistent gap remains a difference in what each fund's investors are told.

      信息差异。各机构对借款人掌握的信息可能不同，例如其中一方担任代理行或进入贷款人委员会；申报文件不记录这些角色。但所比较的估值日期相同，持续存在的差距依然意味着各基金投资者获得的信息不同。

      Exits and restructurings. Loans that leave the schedule may have been repaid at par, so they are never counted as defaults; Section 4.7 reports bounds. A restructuring is inferred when a lender newly holds equity in a borrower while its debt shrinks by more than a quarter, is marked below 90 or disappears. This captures debt-for-equity exchanges such as Pluralsight's but may miss restructurings that only extend maturities or switch interest to PIK.

      退出与重组。从持仓表消失的贷款可能已按面值偿还，因此从不计为违约；4.7 节给出了上下界。当某机构新持有某借款人的股权，同时其债务缩减超过四分之一、估值低于 90 或消失时，推断发生了重组。这能识别 Pluralsight 式的债转股，但可能遗漏仅延长期限或改为 PIK 的重组。

      Coverage and independence. The sample covers BDCs whose filings reconcile; most smaller BDCs do so only from mid-2025, so time-series results rest mainly on the larger filers, and ten BDCs, including FS KKR, one of the largest, are absent. Events and matched loan-quarters are not independent: the same borrower can appear at several lenders and in several quarters, which is why lender-level intervals are clustered by borrower. Private funds, insurers and CLOs, which hold much of the market, disclose less or nothing at the loan level.

      Named firms. Lenders and borrowers are named because the figures come from their public filings. The companies have not reviewed this draft; before publication each named BDC should be sent its figures and the linked filings and given the opportunity to respond, and responses will be reported here.

      覆盖范围与独立性。样本仅含申报能通过核对的 BDC；多数小型 BDC 自 2025 年中起才通过，因此时间序列结果主要依赖大型申报方，且有 10 家 BDC（包括规模最大的机构之一 FS KKR）缺席。事件与匹配观测并不独立：同一借款人可能出现在多家机构和多个季度，因此机构层面的区间按借款人聚类。持有市场相当份额的私募基金、保险公司和 CLO 在逐笔层面披露更少或不披露。

      关于具名机构。文中提及的放款机构和借款人均来自其公开申报。相关公司尚未审阅本稿；发表前应向每家具名 BDC 发送其数据与原始文件链接并给予回应机会，回应将在此处列出。

      6Conclusion结论

      Private-credit marks are neither fictitious nor uniformly slow. Lenders agree closely on performing loans; most losses are recognized over several quarters rather than at default, though a meaningful minority arrive in a single step; and lenders diverge most when a loan's value is most uncertain, without either side being systematically right. The dollar effect on any one fund is small today. What matters is the timing of the signal: two investors holding the same risk can be told different things for a year or more. Loan-level, cross-lender comparison of the kind presented here costs little, uses only data the SEC already requires, and could be run every quarter.

      私募信贷的估值既非虚构，也并非一律迟缓。各机构对正常贷款的估值高度一致；大部分损失在若干季度内逐步确认，而非等到违约，但也有相当一部分是一步到位的；在贷款价值最不确定时，机构间分歧最大，而任何一方都不系统性地更正确。目前对单只基金的美元影响很小，真正重要的是信号的时点：持有同一风险的两位投资者，可能在一年或更长时间里得到不同的信息。本文所示的逐笔、跨机构比较成本低，只用 SEC 已要求披露的数据，每个季度都可以重复进行。
