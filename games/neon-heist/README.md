# Neon Heist — Los Santitos

A 3D open-world crime sandbox in a single HTML file (~900 KB). Same stack as the other demos in this repo:
Three.js + esbuild, no external assets — the city, cars, pedestrians, sky, sound and radio are all generated by code.

## Run

```bash
cd neon-heist
npm install        # three, esbuild
node build.mjs     # -> dist/index.html (open it in a browser)
```

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move on foot / drive |
| Mouse | Look (click the game to capture the pointer; right-drag also looks) |
| Left click | Shoot |
| E / F | Enter, exit or hijack a vehicle |
| Shift · Space | Sprint · Jump / handbrake |
| Hold Q / Tab | Weapon wheel (slows time) — also 1–8 and the mouse wheel |
| Right-click + aim | Hold people up — then press E to rob them |
| E | Talk to people · enter shops · hijack cars |
| C · H · R · L | Camera (chase / far / hood) · Horn · Radio · Cop siren |
| O | Ambient occlusion on/off (off by default, GPU heavy) |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| ~ (backquote) | Cheat menu (also a CHEATS button on the pause screen): god mode, infinite ammo, all weapons, +$100k, never wanted, spawn a car, heal, day/night, teleport to objective, start any rampage |

## What's in it

- **City** — 10×10 block grid (1.2 km²): downtown towers with window-lit facades, parks with fountains, street trees, lamp posts, crosswalks. Buildings, roads and sidewalks are merged into a handful of draw calls.
- **Driving** — arcade car physics with drift and handbrake, body roll/pitch, 6 vehicle types (sedan, sports car, taxi, van, truck, police cruiser), crash damage, smoke, fire and explosions.
- **Traffic & pedestrians** — lane-following AI with intersection turns and avoidance; pedestrians walk the sidewalks, cross streets, flee from gunfire and can be hijacked out of cars.
- **Wanted system** — 1–5 stars. Cops only react to crimes they can see; cruisers chase you, officers pile out and shoot, and at 4+ stars a helicopter with a searchlight joins in. Break line of sight for ~10 s to lose them. Get caught on foot and you're busted.
- **Weapons** — pistol, SMG, shotgun, rocket launcher (limited ammo, found in crates). Crosshair-accurate third-person aiming.
- **Jobs & pickups** — random courier jobs (timed deliveries), cash drops, health and rocket pickups.
- **Day / night** — 8-minute cycle with sky shader, stars, moon, shadows, street lights, headlight beams and glowing windows.
- **Audio** — procedural engine (with gear shifts), gunfire, explosions, sirens, helicopter thump and a generative radio station. No audio files.

## Story & jobs

- **Story** — five missions from Vinnie at the docks (yellow ★): *Fresh Start* (courier), *Hot Property* (steal a car with an alarm), *Corner Store* (rob a 24/7 and escape), *Loose Ends* (stop an informant fleeing to Police HQ) and *Skyway Score* (destroy an armored truck on the Skyway, grab the cash, survive 4 stars). Each one unlocks something: side jobs, better guns at Ammu-Nation, the safehouse.
- **Side activities** — courier jobs (sometimes fragile cargo), steal-to-order, the Skyway time trial (gold/silver/bronze), store robberies, three **rampages** (red markers: borrowed SMG / rocket launcher / bat, a quota and a clock) and **bounties** from the board in the Police HQ lobby.
- **GPS** — the minimap draws a route along the streets (and up the Skyway ramps) to the current objective; map icons stay upright as the map turns.
- **Wanted system** — crimes add heat that escalates up to 5 stars (helicopter at 4+). Cops need line of sight; stars flash while they've lost you. Unarmed at 1–2 stars, cops try to arrest you. Pay'n'Spray costs $150 per star and only works out of sight.
- **Progress saves** to the browser (money, weapons, armor, story) — "New game" on the pause screen erases it. Respawn at the hospital when wasted, at Police HQ when busted.

## World

- **Districts** — downtown towers, mid-rise shopping streets, suburban houses with gardens/pools, an industrial dock with warehouses/containers/tanks, parks with a lake, playground and basketball court, a central plaza with market stalls, parking lots, gas stations, a stadium, a hospital and a police HQ.
- **Skyway** — a drivable elevated expressway with end ramps plus mid-city slip ramps on both sides, bridges over every cross street, and its own fast traffic.
- **Traffic lights** — every junction cycles green/amber/red; AI drivers brake for the stop line and queue patiently behind each other (cops in pursuit and fleeing cars ignore them).
- **Street furniture** — hydrants (hit one with a car for a water jet), litter bins, bus shelters with lit adverts, and a street lamp every ~60 m.
- **Enterable shops** — Ammu-Nation (buy weapons/ammo), 24/7 market, Burger Shot, Velvet & Co (clothes), a safehouse apartment (sleep until morning), the hospital, The Tipsy Taxi bar (drinks make the world sway, pool for $50 bets) and the Police HQ lobby (pay off a wanted level, buy armor, take bounties). The street freezes while you are inside — hide in a shop to lose the cops.
- **Pay'n'Spray** — drive in to repair, respray and shake a wanted level.
- **People** — pedestrians chat, react to cars, guns and crimes with speech bubbles; aim a gun at someone and they put their hands up — press E to rob them.
- **Weapons** — bat, pistol, SMG, shotgun, assault rifle, sniper (scoped), rocket launcher and grenades, with a radial weapon wheel.
- **Radio** — soft procedural pads/jazz that only play inside vehicles (R to switch station).

## Visuals

All textures are painted procedurally at start-up (≈0 bytes shipped):

- **PBR materials** — albedo + normal + packed roughness/metal maps for asphalt (tyre tracks, worn paint, puddles), paving, brick / concrete / glass-curtain-wall facades (three styles), shop fronts with neon signs, roofs and grass.
- **Lighting** — image-based lighting captured live from the procedural sky (cars and glass reflect the real sun/clouds), cascaded soft shadows, sun/moon, street lamps that actually light the road, pavement and lower facades (analytic, no extra lights), per-window lit offices with fake rooms behind the glass (interior mapping), height fog, car headlight spotlight, helicopter searchlight.
- **Post-processing** — bloom, filmic tone mapping, SMAA, vignette, subtle chromatic aberration and film grain. Optional GTAO (key O). Quality auto-degrades on slow GPUs.
- **Models** — lofted car bodies (rounded nose/tail, tumblehome, smooth shading) with clear-coat paint, wheels, bumpers, mirrors, plates and contact shadows; pedestrians with knees, faces, varied builds, long hair and skirts; leaf-card trees. Models are merged into a few draw calls each.

## Source layout

```
src/
├── main.js        # bootstrap, game states, main loop
├── state.js       # shared game context (G)
├── city.js        # world generation, merged meshes, collision queries, minimap texture
├── textures.js    # procedural canvas textures (facades, roads, sidewalks, decals...)
├── sky.js         # day/night cycle, sky dome, sun/moon, fog, shadows
├── vehicles.js    # car models, physics, damage, collisions
├── traffic.js     # traffic spawning + lane/turn/chase AI, red-light stopping
├── signals.js     # junction phases + instanced traffic lights
├── shaderfx.js    # street-lamp lighting, interior-mapped windows, height fog
├── peds.js        # humanoid model + pedestrian AI
├── player.js      # on-foot controller, weapons, entering/exiting cars
├── combat.js      # bullets, rockets, explosions, aim ray-march
├── police.js      # wanted level, cops, helicopter
├── pickups.js     # pickups + courier missions
├── districts.js   # houses / industrial / plaza / parking / gas / stadium / hospital / police / parks
├── viaduct.js     # elevated expressway (ramps, deck, pillars, surface height)
├── interiors.js   # enterable shop interiors
├── shop.js        # shop menu
├── speech.js      # pedestrian speech bubbles + dialogue lines
├── wheel.js       # weapon wheel + weapon pictograms
├── effects.js     # GPU point-sprite particles, decals, tracers, camera shake
├── camera.js      # third-person / chase / hood cameras with building clipping
├── audio.js       # WebAudio synth + generative radio
├── hud.js         # DOM HUD + rotating minimap
└── input.js       # keyboard, mouse, pointer lock
```
