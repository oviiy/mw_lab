TINY GODS

Every match is an act of nature.
Guide a tribe of tiny people from standing stones to castles, cities and the stars.

How to Play

Swap two neighbouring tiles to align three or more. Every element you match acts upon your world. Each move is one day.

The five elements

Rain raises the water in the land. Too much floods the fields.
Sun turns water into food. No water, no harvest. Weak in winter.
Earth raises buildings, which house more people.
Fire defeats threats. With none about, it forges and helps building.
Spirit brings faith and Divine Power.

Seven ages

Faith and population carry you from the Stone Age through the Medieval, Industrial and Modern ages to the Future. The elements take new forms as you advance (Rain becomes Irrigation, Steam, the Water Grid), but their roles stay the same.

Chain reactions

Match four for a line blast, an L or T for a bomb, five for a prism orb. From the Iron Age on, power tiles appear on their own, more often with every age. Chains multiply yields and score.

Score & ascension

Everything you do adds to your score. Reach the Future Age to win, then keep reigning: each Ascension raises the stakes and the multiplier.
