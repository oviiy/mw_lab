Skerry

Raise basalt columns from a cold northern sea and grow a little island, one tile at a time.

How to play

You get a stack of tiles. Grow the best-fitting island you can before it runs out. Each rank you reach wins 2 more tiles and brings new life to the island.

  • Place each tile beside your island. Every side that matches its neighbour scores 10.
  • Your tile turns to its best fit for each spot you point at. Turn it yourself to try something else. Press Hint to see the best spot.
  • Water must always meet water. Red marks show the sides that can't touch.
  • Match both sides of a tile with two neighbours for a snug fit. Match three or more for a perfect fit, which also wins an extra tile. Good fits in a row build a streak that multiplies their points, up to ×2.
  • Some tiles bring a request. Grow that area to the number of tiles shown to win 3 more tiles. If the area gets closed off, the request is lost.
  • Every isle has a type with a twist of its own: a Lake isle scores more for lakes, a Tidal isle lets streaks run higher, and so on. It's shown as the isle rises and beside your score.
  • Seal off an area of four or more tiles, with no open edges left, for 10 points per tile.
