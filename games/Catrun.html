How to play

Run, jump, slide — and show off. Risky moves = big points.

CONTROLS

⬆️
Jump — SPACE ↑ W · 📱 right thumb. Tap = hop, hold = big leap. Press again in mid-air to double jump.
⬇️
Slide / Dive — ↓ S · 📱 left thumb. Hold to slide under things. In the air it's a fast dive.
⏸️
Pause — P / ESC or the ❚❚ button. Left-handed? Swap thumbs in ⚙️ Settings.

TRICKS — FILL THE STYLE BAR

⏱️
Last Second — jump at the very last moment before a hazard or pit edge.
⚡
Ground Skim — fall almost to the floor, then double jump just before landing.
🪒
Close Call / Razor Dodge — pass a hazard by a whisker. The closer, the more points.
🛷
Limbo → Limbo Hop — slide under a hazard, then jump straight out of the slide.
🐾
Stomp Chain — stomp enemies back-to-back without landing. Dive onto one for a Pound.
🌀
Air Combo — pull 2+ tricks in one jump. Full Arc — grab every coin of an arc.
🌟
A full Style bar triggers FEVER: x2 points & coins, coin rain and auto-magnet. Getting hit drains the bar.

MODES

🗺️
Adventure — 5 worlds × 4 stages, each world has new enemies and a boss (stomp its glowing weak spot!). Earn up to 3★ per stage: finish, collect 60% coins, take no damage.
♾️
Endless — how far can you go? Every zone adds new enemies.

PROGRESSION

✨
Talents are permanent: extra lives, glide, triple jump, second chance and more. Strong ones need ★ stars from Adventure.
🎯
Missions pay coins. Every 3 missions = new Rank = +10% score forever.
